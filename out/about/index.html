About Chrisko Home Automation

Chrisko Home Automation is a Toronto-based furniture assembly company serving the Greater Toronto Area. Despite the name, what we do is refreshingly simple: we build furniture, properly. Beds, wardrobes, dining sets, offices, home gyms and gazebos — assembled by people who do it every day, with the right tools and zero corner-cutting.

We started with a simple observation: Toronto households were losing weekends to flat-pack instructions, stripping cam locks on expensive furniture, and leaving tall dressers unanchored. Meanwhile the alternative — random gig apps — meant strangers with unknown skills and no insurance. We built the company we wanted to hire: trained local assemblers, $2M insurance, firm written quotes and a real warranty.

Today we assemble furniture in downtown condos, post-war family homes and new builds across 12 GTA communities — from single MALM beds to whole-house move-ins, office fit-outs with COIs, and hardtop gazebos built in a day. Every job follows the same standard, whether it's a $199 visit or a full commercial rollout.
