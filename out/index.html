Furniture assembly FAQs

Still have a question? Call us at +1 (437) 870-1113.

How much does furniture assembly cost in Toronto?

House visits start at $199+HST and downtown condo visits at $249+HST, covering most single large items like a bed, dresser or desk. Multi-item visits are quoted firmly upfront — typically $350–$600 for a full room. The price we quote is the price you pay.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Toronto and the entire Greater Toronto Area: North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Oakville, Ajax and Whitby. No travel charges anywhere in our service area.

What furniture brands do you assemble?

All of them — IKEA (PAX, MALM, BILLY, BESTÅ, HEMNES, SEKTION), Wayfair, Structube, Cozey, West Elm, Costco, Amazon, CB2, EQ3, Article and more. If it arrives flat-packed with instructions, we build it.

Are you insured, and do you warranty your work?

Yes on both. We carry $2M liability insurance — certificates available for condo property managers — and every assembly includes a written 12-month workmanship warranty. If our work needs attention, we come back and make it right.

How fast can you come out?

Same-day and next-day appointments are available across most of the GTA. Call +1 (437) 870-1113 with your item list and we'll find the earliest window, including evenings and Saturdays.

Do you take away the boxes and packaging?

We break down all cardboard and packaging and stack it neatly for your recycling pickup at no charge. Full haul-away can be arranged for a small fee where buildings require it.

Will you anchor my furniture to the wall?

Yes — anti-tip anchoring to studs is included for dressers, wardrobes, bookcases and other tall items. It's a safety essential in homes with children, and we never skip it.

What if parts are missing or damaged?

We do a full parts inventory before assembly and flag issues immediately — with photos and exact part numbers for your reorder. We carry common spare hardware, and return visits to finish a build are discounted.

Can you take over an assembly I started myself?

Absolutely — partial DIY rescues are some of our most common calls. We assess what's done, safely correct any mistakes, and finish the build at standard rates. No judgment, ever.
