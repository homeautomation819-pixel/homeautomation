Commercial and home office, both covered

For businesses, we handle full office fit-outs: benching and cubicle systems for ten to fifty seats, boardroom and meeting-room tables, reception desks, storage walls and break-room furniture. We coordinate with your property manager — certificates of insurance, elevator bookings and after-hours access are routine for us — and we can work evenings or weekends so your team arrives Monday to a finished office.

For home offices — and after 2020, half the GTA has one — we assemble sit-stand desks, ergonomic chairs, shelving and monitor setups in condos and houses alike. A proper home office build, anchored and levelled, usually takes us about an hour.

What businesses get with every install

Commercial assembly has requirements residential work doesn't. Ours is built for it:

  • Certificate of insurance (COI) issued to your property manager before the install date
  • After-hours, early-morning and weekend scheduling to avoid disrupting operations
  • Batch assembly lines for multi-unit orders — chairs and desks assembled in sequence, not one at a time
  • Cable-management routing on desks and benching as we build
  • Packaging consolidation and removal coordination with building loading docks
  • A single point of contact and a firm written quote — no hourly billing

Office furniture assembly pricing in Toronto

Single home-office setups (desk plus chair) fit our $199+HST house visit or $249+HST condo visit. Commercial work is quoted per project: a 10-person workstation rollout typically runs $1,200–$2,500 depending on systems and cable management; boardrooms and reception areas are quoted individually. Volume pricing applies automatically to multi-unit orders — the per-item rate drops as item count rises.

How much does office furniture assembly cost in Toronto?

Home-office setups fit our standard $199+HST house or $249+HST condo visits. Commercial projects are quoted per scope — a 10-station rollout typically runs $1,200–$2,500 with volume pricing. Always a firm written quote first.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance for our building?

Yes — we carry $2M liability insurance and routinely issue COIs to property managers and condo corporations before install day.

Do you work after hours or weekends?

Yes. Evening and weekend installs are standard for commercial clients so assembly never disrupts your business hours.

Can you assemble 30 desks and chairs in one visit?

Yes — multi-unit orders are our specialty. We run assembly-line builds and bring crew sizes matched to the scope and your deadline.
