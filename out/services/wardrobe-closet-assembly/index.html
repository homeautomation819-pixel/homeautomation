IKEA PAX — our signature build

PAX is the wardrobe system Toronto buys more than any other, and it's the one that punishes DIY hardest. Frames must be dead square or sliding doors rack and bind. Ceilings under 96 inches mean frames can't be stood up after flat assembly — they must be built upright. Floors in older Toronto homes are rarely level, so shimming and levelling come before anchoring, and anchoring into studs is non-negotiable for a 7-foot frame full of clothing.

We build PAX with KOMPLEMENT drawers, pull-out rails, trouser racks, interior lighting and both door types — and we measure your ceiling and wall conditions at quoting so there are no surprises on build day.

Common problems we solve

Almost always an out-of-square frame. We square before doors ever go on.

Wardrobe leaning off the wall

Interior fittings in the wrong spots

Frame built flat, ceiling too low

The classic PAX trap. We measure at quoting and build upright when required.

Wardrobe & Closet Assembly FAQs

Still have a question? Call us at +1 (437) 870-1113.

How much does wardrobe assembly cost in Toronto?

Single armoires fit our $199+HST house visit. Two-frame PAX systems run $250–$350; large sliding-door walls $400–$600. Downtown condos from $249+HST. Always a firm quote before we start.

Can you assemble an IKEA PAX with sliding doors?

Yes — it's our signature build. We square, level and anchor the frames, fit KOMPLEMENT interiors, and adjust the doors until they glide silently.

My ceilings are 8 feet — can PAX still work?

Yes. Frames are 79 or 93 inches; we measure your ceiling at quoting and build frames upright when there isn't clearance to stand them up.

Do wardrobes need to be anchored to the wall?

Absolutely — a loaded wardrobe is a tip-over hazard. Anchoring to studs is included in every wardrobe build we do.

How long does wardrobe assembly take?

A single armoire about an hour; a two-frame PAX two to three hours; full closet systems a half to full day.

Can you design the closet layout too?

We help you configure interiors to your clothing and storage, and can work from an IKEA or third-party planner design you've already made.
