Mounted right, on any wall

Every mount starts with the wall, not the TV. On drywall we locate studs and set lag bolts properly — never drywall anchors for a TV. On brick and concrete (common in Toronto lofts and condos) we use masonry bits and sleeve anchors rated for the load. Above fireplaces we check heat exposure and viewing height honestly — sometimes the honest advice is a different wall.

We install fixed, tilting and full-motion brackets sized to your TV's VESA pattern and weight, level to the millimetre. Cable options range from a clean surface raceway to in-wall concealment kits with recessed power where code allows — we'll show you both and quote the difference upfront.

More than TVs

The same precision applies to everything heavy on your walls:

  • Soundbars mounted and paired under the TV
  • Mirrors — including heavy gym and statement mirrors — anchored to studs or masonry
  • Artwork, gallery walls and picture ledges, levelled and spaced
  • Curtain rods and blinds brackets, including tall condo windows
  • Floating shelves and wall-mounted storage

TV Mounting & Installation FAQs

Still have a question? Call us at +1 (437) 870-1113.

How much does TV mounting cost in Toronto?

Standard drywall mounting runs $149–$199+HST with your bracket. Brick, concrete and above-fireplace installs run $199–$299. In-wall cable concealment adds $100–$150 where the wall allows.

Can you mount a TV on a concrete condo wall?

Yes — concrete and brick are routine for us. We use masonry anchors rated well beyond the TV's weight and bring dust control for the drilling.

Do you supply the bracket?

We can supply quality fixed, tilting or full-motion brackets matched to your TV, or install the bracket you already own — either is quoted upfront.

Can you hide the cables inside the wall?

In most drywall installations, yes, using in-wall rated kits. Where code or wall construction doesn't allow it, we install paintable raceway that blends in cleanly.

Is mounting a TV above a fireplace a good idea?

Sometimes — it depends on heat output and viewing height. We'll assess honestly at the visit and suggest alternatives if the neck strain isn't worth it.

How long does TV mounting take?

A standard mount takes about an hour including cable management and cleanup. Fireplace or masonry installs run up to two.
