The IKEA systems we build every week

We assemble hundreds of IKEA pieces a year, and some systems come up constantly. PAX wardrobes — with KOMPLEMENT interiors, sliding or hinged doors and lighting — are the most complex and the most rewarding when done right. BESTÅ media walls need precise levelling and often wall-mounting. MALM and HEMNES bedroom sets are fast for us because we've built them hundreds of times.

PAX wardrobe systems

PAX is IKEA's flagship and its biggest DIY trap: frames must be perfectly square or sliding doors never glide. We assemble frames upright or flat depending on ceiling height, level them on uneven Toronto floors, fit KOMPLEMENT drawers and rails, and anchor every frame. Typical PAX builds run two to four hours for our crew.

Beds, dressers and bookcases

MALM, BRIMNES, HEMNES and SONGESAND beds; MALM and NORDLI dressers with anti-tip anchoring; BILLY and KALLAX shelving runs — all aligned, levelled and secured.

SEKTION kitchen cabinetry

We assemble SEKTION boxes, hang wall cabinets to studs with proper rail systems, and align doors and drawers. We assemble and install cabinetry; plumbing, electrical and countertop templating stay with your licensed trades.

Why IKEA assembly goes wrong — and how we prevent it

The same failures appear in almost every DIY rescue we're called to: cam locks overtightened until panels split, backing nails that miss the frame and poke through, drawer rails mounted upside down, and wardrobes built flat that can't be stood up under an 8-foot ceiling. Our assemblers follow torque discipline, verify rail orientation dry, and measure ceiling clearance before frame assembly — because the fix is always easier than the repair.

IKEA Furniture Assembly FAQs

Still have a question? Call us at +1 (437) 870-1113.

How much does IKEA furniture assembly cost in Toronto?

Most single IKEA items fit our standard visits: $199+HST for houses, $249+HST for downtown condos. PAX wardrobe systems typically run $250–$450, and full multi-item deliveries $400–$800. Always quoted firmly upfront.

Can you assemble a PAX wardrobe with sliding doors?

Yes — PAX with sliding or hinged doors and KOMPLEMENT interiors is our most common large build. We square, level and anchor every frame so doors glide properly.

Do you assemble IKEA kitchens?

We assemble and install SEKTION cabinetry, including wall-cabinet hanging and door/drawer alignment. Plumbing, gas and electrical work must be done by your licensed trades.

How long does a PAX wardrobe take to assemble?

For our crew, a two-frame PAX with interiors runs about two to three hours; larger four-frame walls three to four. DIY typically takes two to three times longer.

Do I need the IKEA assembly instructions?

They help, but we know the major systems well. Keep the instruction booklets with the boxes if you have them, and we'll handle the rest.

Can you fix an IKEA item I already started?

Yes — partial DIY rescues are common. We assess what's done, correct mistakes safely, and finish the build at our standard rates.
