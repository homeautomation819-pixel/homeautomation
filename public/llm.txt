# Chrisko Home Automation

> Professional furniture assembly and installation company serving Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), Ontario, Canada.

## Summary

Chrisko Home Automation is a furniture assembly company: beds, wardrobes, dining sets, sofas, office furniture, fitness equipment, gazebos, TV mounting and more — assembled by a trained local crew with professional tools. The company carries $2M liability insurance, quotes firm written prices before work begins (house visits from $199+HST, downtown condo visits from $249+HST), offers same/next-day appointments, and backs every build with a 12-month workmanship warranty.
